Reward Screen Odds

A logistic regression fit separately for every character/card pair offered at the reward screen, controlling for HP ratio, floor, relic count, and ascension level at the moment of the pick. The 579 cards below are the ones where picking the card cleared four bars: the fit converged, the card belongs to the character it's credited to (rather than a rare off-class Prismatic Shard pick), the effect survived Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction, and the odds ratio moved outside [0.9, 1.1] — not just statistically significant, but large enough to matter.
